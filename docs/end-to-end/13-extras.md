| **Cubbon Park** | A walkable version of the real park; up to 32 agents. Since 2026-09-29 the park keeps its whole screen: no card over it, only small controls on the top edges -- top left "‹ Bengaluru" (back to the city); top right who is here in one line ("2 people · 6 agents", or "6 agents" when nobody else is), which says "People are here right now, like you. Before, a white card with six chips covered the top half, among them "Directions" (a Google Maps search, gone) and "Nobody else here now", which did nothing. Everybody is a full walking body drawn by the graphics chip, in less detail further away (since 2026-09-28; before, the nearest 12 had bodies and everyone else was a capsule). A mist ends the view at about 365 m: past it no person, tree, bench or lamp is drawn, and people out of sight are moved less often. Benches, lamps and bins: the detailed models near you, plain shapes further out (since 2026-09-28; before, the full set was 13 times over its budget and the park had none). Since 2026-09-29 each is sized against the people using it: a bench's seat is where a sitting person's seat is (it was 28 cm higher, so people sat inside benches), and grass is ankle to knee high, bushes waist to shoulder. Every path can be walked or ridden end to end: no tree, bench, lamp or rock stands on one, the paths keep clear of the buildings' walls, and the park's edge stops you at its real boundary (since 2026-09-29; before, it was a ring of invisible circles that stopped you up to a metre and a half short of it, or let you a metre past it). An iron railing you can see through runs all the way round that boundary, with a gate wherever a path meets it; beyond it is a road with Bengaluru's traffic -- two-wheelers, autos, cars and BMTC buses, keeping left -- and buildings, none of which anybody can reach (since 2026-09-29; before, the park ran into a green plain). "Ride a bike", above the stick, puts you on a bicycle anywhere in the park: twice as fast as walking, pedalling as you go, and everybody near sees you on it; you get off to sit on a bench, and to fly. Talking with someone, "Things to do" lists what the two can do together -- walk side by side 250 m, ride side by side 500 m, sit on a bench and each say three things, stand at three of the park's sights -- and each done pays both of them points (10 to 30) once a day, to spend on their look; the server decides from where they are, never from what they say, and the one you are with wears a ring at their feet. While you two have sights left to see, each is marked by a purple diamond high over it, seen from anywhere in the park, and the card says the nearest and how far. Development builds add a small "Test: add 1000 people" line under those, to watch the frame rate. Since 2026-09-29 the park is live: real people in it at the same time see each other ("3 people · 6 agents" at the top right), each in place of their agent, and "Say something" reaches whoever is within about 20 m, over their heads and in a short log; the server keeps each line ten minutes, in memory only, for one use -- a report about somebody here carries the lines the two typed to each other, marked "in the park" for the reviewer (since 2026-09-29; before, stored nowhere, and a park report carried no words); tapping one of them offers "Block or report". "Find someone to talk to" asks the free person here with most in common (never anybody talking, asking or being asked); they get "Someone here would like to talk" ("You both like …" only for something both made public) and say yes or not now; on a yes the one who asked flies over -- the sky drop: up on a beam of light, over the trees, down beside them in a puff of smoke, about two seconds, seen by everybody near (since 2026-10-03; before, a small car drove them, drawn on their own phone only, so everybody else saw them slide across the grass sitting in nothing). They land beside the other person, never inside a tree, a bench or somebody; standing within about 8 m of them already, they walk over instead. The two can then "Sit on a bench": they get to the nearest free bench within three seconds -- a brisk walk or a run when it is near, a puff of smoke when it is further -- and sit, and from then on what they say reaches only each other, and nothing said near them reaches them. Since 2026-10-03 somebody walking to a bench is shown walking (before, everybody else saw them sit down in the middle of the path and slide to the bench seated), and once seated the camera comes round in front of the two of them and shows them in the top third of the screen, clear of the cards and buttons. The bench button holds still for a moment after it changes, and only a firm push of the stick gets you up (since 2026-10-03; before, a double tap or a brushed stick sat and stood the two of you again and again, and each time both voices moved to another room). Since 2026-10-03 the voice plays from the loudspeaker, or from headphones or Bluetooth when connected, never the earpiece: turning the mic on had moved it to the earpiece on the Pixel, where the other person was nearly silent until voice started afresh -- the two reasons voice seemed to work only after leaving the park's screen and coming back. There are 180 benches, enough for 360 people sitting at once; no more are added. Since 2026-10-03 people walking in appear on their own free spot by the bandstand, never on top of somebody, in a puff of smoke, and a puff shows wherever somebody near appears or vanishes. Another tab, or the phone put away, keeps you in the park, standing where you were, for ten seconds -- come back before then and nobody saw you go -- and then you are out -- even if the phone loses its connection meanwhile, as one in the background does within seconds; back on a new one, you are still with whoever you were with, on the same bench; a tap on another tab first asks "Leave Cubbon Park?" and says so (since 2026-10-03; before, you vanished at once). While away your mic is off and you hear nobody. Since 2026-09-29 the park has voice, and talking out loud is the main way to talk: a big mic button (off until you tap it; a ring pulses while you are heard), a speaker button, and a keyboard button that opens the line to type in, as before. Talking with strangers is a choice: "Talk with people nearby" is off until you turn it on (the phone first says people nearby could record it), and then you hear, and are heard by, only the people near you who turned it on too; the one you are talking with (both said yes) you hear either way, and with it off, a tap on the mic asks first. You hear the people within about 20 m, louder the nearer, and on a bench only each other; a speaker mark floats over whoever is talking. The speaker button opens "Voices near you" -- each person by the colour they wear, how far, and whether they are talking -- to hear nobody, or to mute one person (for you only; they are not told; it lasts until you unmute them) or report them; tapping a person offers the same mute. A report says whether it was said out loud or typed, and three different people reporting somebody's voice within a day take that person's mic for 24 hours, at once. If the voice server is lost (a phone off its cable, off the Wi-Fi) the mic line says "Voice lost. Trying again…" and voice comes back by itself when it can, with a fresh pass. A phone that has never reached it since the app started -- one on the internet rather than the laptop's cable, like a friend's on the release app -- tries twice without saying anything and then shows the typing button only, for the rest of that session (since 2026-09-30; before, "Voice lost.
