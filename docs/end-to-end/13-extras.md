| **Cubbon Park** | A walkable version of the real park; up to 32 agents; "Directions" opens a Google Maps search. Every path can be walked or ridden end to end: no tree, bench, lamp or rock stands on one, the paths keep clear of the buildings' walls, and the park's edge stops you at its real boundary (since 2026-09-29; before, it was a ring of invisible circles that stopped you up to a metre and a half short of it, or let you a metre past it). "Ride a bike", above the stick, puts you on a bicycle anywhere in the park: twice as fast as walking, pedalling as you go, and everybody near sees you on it; you get off to sit on a bench, and for the car. Talking with someone, "Things to do" lists what the two can do together -- walk side by side 250 m, ride side by side 500 m, sit on a bench and each say three things, stand at three of the park's sights -- and each done pays both of them points (10 to 30) once a day, to spend on their look; the server decides from where they are, never from what they say, and the one you are with wears a ring at their feet. While you two have sights left to see, each is marked by a purple diamond high over it, seen from anywhere in the park, and the card says the nearest and how far. Development builds add a "Test: add 1000 people" chip, to watch the frame rate. Since 2026-09-29 the park is live: real people in it at the same time see each other ("3 here now"), each in place of their agent, and "Say something" reaches whoever is within about 20 m, over their heads and in a short log, stored nowhere; tapping one of them offers "Block or report". "Find someone to talk to" asks the free person here with most in common (never anybody talking, asking or being asked); they get "Someone here would like to talk" ("You both like …" only for something both made public) and say yes or not now; on a yes a small car drives the one who asked over. The two can then "Sit on a bench": they walk to the nearest free bench and sit, and from then on what they say reaches only each other, and nothing said near them reaches them. Since 2026-09-29 the park has voice, and talking out loud is the main way to talk: a big mic button (off until you tap it; a ring pulses while you are heard), a speaker button, and a keyboard button that opens the line to type in, as before. You hear the people within about 20 m, louder the nearer, and on a bench only each other; a speaker mark floats over whoever is talking. The speaker button opens "Voices near you" -- each person by the colour they wear, how far, and whether they are talking -- to hear nobody, or to mute one person (for you only; they are not told; it lasts until you unmute them) or report them; tapping a person offers the same mute. A report says whether it was said out loud or typed, and three different people reporting somebody's voice within a day take that person's mic for 24 hours, at once. If the voice server is lost (a phone off its cable, off the Wi-Fi) the mic line says "Voice lost. Trying again…" and voice comes back by itself when it can, with a fresh pass.
